The Nine
Lives of Ash

Nine lives. One hungry Vole.
Three paws per turn.

How to play

  1. You are Ash (10 HP). Face The Vole (5 HP).
  2. Each turn you get 3 paw placements. You open with 3 Ferocity.
  3. Scratch: free, once per turn, 1 damage. Pounce: 2 Ferocity cost, 3 charges, 4 damage.
  4. Slink: 1 Shadow, 3 charges, Block 3. Purr: 1 Guile, 2 charges, heals 2 HP per turn over 2 turns, skills and Concentrate refused while channeling, Slip Away stays open, broken by damage. Loaf: 1 Guile, 2 charges, Block 5, guards the hand, ends the turn.
  5. Concentrate (2 per fight): give up the whole turn to will 1 spent energy back. Slip Away: free, but the Vole gets its telegraphed move as a parting shot.
  6. The Vole telegraphs Hold Very Still (nothing) and Dart for Cover (takes 1 energy — Loaf guards). You draw 1 Ferocity per turn. Press End Turn to let The Vole act. Reduce it to 0 HP to win. Lose all HP to lose a life.

Note: this browser prototype simplifies charge-to-power and is not exact card parity.

Unofficial browser prototype. No external assets or network. Best at 506×899 portrait.
