3. State that only two tracked screenshots were available and that generated tour images were not remotely verified.
4. Preserve “no checkout” methodology and direct GitHub/API/raw links.
5. Treat later chapter content and minigames as design/data or planned scope where the source does not prove a finished public build.
6. Prefer repository-backed numbers over invented market or player claims.
