## Play

- Open https://lumenrift.wbg.gg/ in a desktop or mobile browser and start a run from the title screen.
- Between nights, pick a structure from the bottom bar and place it on the grid; drag to paint walls and traps, and watch the glowing trail showing the shadows' live path.
- Read the left panel to preview the next night's shadows and rift openings, then call the night early for bonus aether if ready.
- Spend aether on towers, upgrades, Harvesters on crystal veins, and Beacon ascensions that unlock higher structure tiers.
- Unleash the Beacon Nova (V key or on-screen button) when shadows reach the light; survive all 30 nights including the Eclipse, or continue into endless night.

## Mechanics

- Central Beacon defense across 30 nights plus endless mode, with themed nights, boss nights every 10th night, and a dual-boss Eclipse finale
- Live flow-field pathfinding on a 37x23 grid so walls and towers re-route ground shadows; placement that would seal a rift path is refused
- 13 structures in 5 Beacon-ascension tiers with 3 upgrade levels each: walls, traps, economy, aura, chain lightning, ramping beams, and a Sun Obelisk
- 9 shadow types with distinct counters: swarmers, maze-ignoring flyers, wall-smashing brutes, healers, armoured beetles, broodmothers, and 2 bosses
- Crystal-vein Harvester economy, early-call aether bonuses, per-hit and percentage armour model, and four tower targeting priorities
- Beacon Nova area blast, fixed 60 Hz simulation with 1x/2x/3x speed, procedural maps, seeded waves, and three difficulty levels
- Procedural Canvas2D art with dynamic light-map rendering and fully synthesized WebAudio effects plus an adaptive generative score
- 8 languages with automatic system detection, desktop keyboard/mouse and mobile touch with pinch-zoom

Make a polished browser tower-defense game in TypeScript with no engine: a central Beacon defends against 30 nights of escalating shadow waves on procedural maps, with maze-shaping walls, 13 unlockable structures across 5 tiers, 9 enemy types plus bosses, a crystal economy, dynamic code-drawn lighting, synthesized sound and generative music, desktop and mobile touch controls, and multiple languages.
