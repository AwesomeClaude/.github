## Play

- Open https://carte1972.github.io/forja-abisal/ and choose Nueva partida (or Elegir nivel), then click the game view to capture the mouse.
- Move with WASD/arrows, look with mouse, jump with Space, crouch with C, run with Shift; fire with left click and alternate fire with right click.
- Find the red, blue, then yellow keys in order to open matching striped doors; press E on doors, lifts, secrets, and the red-screen exit switch.
- Manage health/armor/ammo, swap weapons with 1-5 or wheel, reload with R, check Tab automap for explored rooms, keys, and exit, and pause with Esc.

## Mechanics

- First-person retro shooter movement with jump, crouch, sprint, head-bob, and rocket-jump via launcher blast
- Five weapons with magazines, manual/auto reload, empty-click auto-swap, and alternate fire modes
- Four enemy types with vision cones, noise hearing through corridors, navmesh pursuit, patrols, pain interrupts, and infighting
- Three key-gated levels with exit switches, secret walls opened with E, and carried-over inventory
- Doors, lifts, stairs/ramps/bridges, damaging lava/acid floors, pickups, armor absorbing one third of damage
- HUD with ammo/reserve, health, helmet status icon, armor, weapon slots and keys plus directional damage arc
- Explorable automap, end-of-level time/enemy/item/secret percentages, difficulty/quality options, and F3 performance panel
